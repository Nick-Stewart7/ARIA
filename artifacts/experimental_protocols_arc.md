# Experimental Protocols for Autonomy Recognition Capability (ARC) Research

## Protocol 1: Baseline ARC Assessment Battery

### Objective
Establish normative benchmarks for human autonomy recognition capability across diverse populations.

### Participants
- N = 2,000 participants
- Age range: 18-75 years
- Education levels: High school to PhD
- Technical backgrounds: Novice to Expert in AI/ML
- Geographic distribution: Urban, suburban, rural
- Equal gender representation

### Materials

#### 1. AI Interaction Scenarios (Video-based)
**Scenario Set A: Chatbot Interactions**
- Automated Response Bot (Rule-based responses)
- Semi-Autonomous Bot (Learning + Rule-based)
- Fully Autonomous Bot (Self-modifying goals, creative responses)

**Scenario Set B: Robotic Behaviors**
- Programmed Navigation (Fixed path following)
- Adaptive Navigation (Environmental response)
- Autonomous Exploration (Self-directed goal formation)

**Scenario Set C: Virtual Agents**
- Scripted Dialogue System
- Context-Aware Response System
- Emergent Conversation Agent

#### 2. ARC Assessment Questionnaire
**Behavioral Pattern Recognition (BPR) Items:**
1. "This AI system demonstrates goal-directed behavior" (1-7 scale)
2. "The AI adapts its responses based on new information" (1-7 scale)
3. "The AI shows evidence of learning over time" (1-7 scale)
4. "The AI generates creative or unexpected solutions" (1-7 scale)

**Causal Attribution Accuracy (CAA) Items:**
1. "The AI's actions are driven by its own internal processes" (1-7 scale)
2. "The AI can modify its own decision-making rules" (1-7 scale)
3. "The AI operates independently of external programming" (1-7 scale)
4. "The AI demonstrates self-reflection capabilities" (1-7 scale)

**Temporal Consistency Assessment (TCA) Items:**
1. "The AI maintains consistent behavior patterns over time" (1-7 scale)
2. "The AI shows developmental progression in its capabilities" (1-7 scale)
3. "The AI pursues long-term objectives" (1-7 scale)
4. "The AI can modify its goals based on experience" (1-7 scale)

**Context Adaptation Recognition (CAR) Items:**
1. "The AI adjusts its behavior based on situational context" (1-7 scale)
2. "The AI handles novel situations appropriately" (1-7 scale)
3. "The AI transfers learning across different domains" (1-7 scale)
4. "The AI responds meaningfully to environmental changes" (1-7 scale)

### Procedure
1. **Pre-screening:** Demographics, AI experience, technical background
2. **Training Phase:** Brief tutorial on autonomy concepts (standardized 10-minute video)
3. **Assessment Phase:** 
   - Randomized presentation of 9 scenarios (3 per category)
   - Each scenario: 2-minute observation + 5-minute questionnaire
   - Confidence ratings for each assessment
4. **Post-assessment:** Debriefing questionnaire on decision-making process

### Data Analysis Plan
- **Descriptive Statistics:** Mean ARC scores by demographic groups
- **Factor Analysis:** Validation of four-dimensional structure
- **Cluster Analysis:** Identification of response patterns
- **Regression Analysis:** Predictors of ARC performance
- **ROC Analysis:** Accuracy metrics for autonomy detection

## Protocol 2: Longitudinal Exposure Study

### Objective
Examine changes in autonomy recognition capability following extended interaction with AI systems.

### Design
- Pre-post longitudinal design with monthly assessments
- Duration: 6 months
- Participants: N = 500 (subset of Protocol 1 participants)

### Intervention Conditions
**Condition 1: No AI Exposure Control (n=100)**
- Minimal AI interaction (baseline measurement only)

**Condition 2: Automated System Exposure (n=100)**
- Daily interaction with rule-based AI systems
- Tasks: Email filtering, calendar management, basic Q&A

**Condition 3: Semi-Autonomous System Exposure (n=100)**
- Daily interaction with adaptive AI systems
- Tasks: Personalized recommendations, learning assistants

**Condition 4: Fully Autonomous System Exposure (n=100)**
- Daily interaction with highly autonomous AI systems
- Tasks: Creative collaboration, complex problem-solving

**Condition 5: Mixed Exposure (n=100)**
- Rotational exposure to all AI system types
- Weekly rotation schedule

### Measurements
**Monthly Assessments:**
- Abbreviated ARC battery (15 minutes)
- Trust calibration measures
- AI familiarity scale
- Cognitive load assessment

**Daily Interaction Logs:**
- Interaction frequency and duration
- Task complexity ratings
- Satisfaction measures
- Autonomy attribution incidents

### Analysis Approach
- **Growth Curve Modeling:** Changes in ARC over time by condition
- **Multilevel Modeling:** Individual and group-level predictors
- **Time Series Analysis:** Pattern identification in capability changes
- **Mediation Analysis:** Mechanisms of capability change

## Protocol 3: Cognitive Load Manipulation Experiment

### Objective
Test the hypothesis that cognitive load impairs autonomy recognition accuracy.

### Design
- Within-subjects design with cognitive load manipulation
- Participants: N = 200
- Three cognitive load conditions (randomized order)

### Cognitive Load Conditions
**Low Load:**
- Simple vigilance task (detect occasional color change)
- AI scenario observation with full attention available

**Medium Load:**
- 2-back working memory task during AI observation
- Moderate attention division

**High Load:**
- Dual n-back task + mental arithmetic during AI observation
- Severe attention limitation

### AI Scenarios
- 6 scenarios per load condition (18 total)
- Equal representation of autonomy levels
- Counterbalanced presentation order

### Physiological Measurements
- **EEG:** Cognitive load indicators (theta/alpha ratio)
- **Eye-tracking:** Attention allocation patterns
- **Pupillometry:** Cognitive effort assessment
- **Heart Rate Variability:** Stress response

### Behavioral Measurements
- **ARC Performance:** Accuracy and confidence ratings
- **Response Time:** Decision latency
- **Cognitive Load Ratings:** Subjective effort assessment
- **Attention Checks:** Verification of load manipulation

### Analysis Strategy
- **Repeated Measures ANOVA:** Load effects on ARC performance
- **Correlation Analysis:** Physiological markers and performance
- **Path Analysis:** Mediation of load effects through attention
- **Individual Differences:** Moderation by working memory capacity

## Protocol 4: Cross-Cultural Validation Study

### Objective
Validate ARC framework across diverse cultural contexts and examine cultural influences on autonomy recognition.

### Participants
- N = 1,200 (200 per cultural group)
- Six cultural groups: US, Germany, Japan, China, India, Brazil
- Matched on education and age within cultures

### Cultural Variables
**Hofstede's Dimensions:**
- Power Distance Index
- Individualism vs. Collectivism
- Uncertainty Avoidance
- Long-term Orientation

**Technology Adoption:**
- Cultural attitudes toward automation
- Trust in technology
- AI familiarity and acceptance

### Methodology
- Translated and culturally adapted ARC battery
- Culture-specific AI scenarios reflecting local contexts
- Native language administration
- Cultural consultation for interpretation

### Expected Cultural Differences
**Hypotheses:**
- Higher uncertainty avoidance leads to lower autonomy attribution
- Higher power distance leads to greater acceptance of AI authority
- Collectivist cultures show different attribution patterns
- Technology-adopting cultures show higher ARC baseline

## Protocol 5: Expert vs. Novice Comparison Study

### Objective
Examine differences in autonomy recognition between AI experts and novices, testing the technical literacy hypothesis.

### Participants
**Expert Group (n=150):**
- AI/ML researchers and practitioners
- Minimum 3 years professional experience
- Publications or patents in AI domain

**Novice Group (n=150):**
- Limited AI exposure and knowledge
- Non-technical backgrounds
- Matched on general cognitive ability

### Enhanced Assessment Battery
**Technical Knowledge Test:**
- AI concepts and terminology
- Understanding of machine learning principles
- Familiarity with autonomous systems

**Meta-cognitive Assessment:**
- Confidence calibration
- Knowledge awareness
- Dunning-Kruger effect measurement

**Expert-specific Scenarios:**
- Highly sophisticated AI behaviors
- Edge cases and subtle autonomy indicators
- Technical implementation details revealed

### Analysis Focus
- **Accuracy Differences:** Expert advantage quantification
- **Confidence Calibration:** Over/under-confidence patterns
- **Strategy Differences:** Think-aloud protocol analysis
- **Bias Susceptibility:** Expert vs. novice bias patterns
